Common Questions

If you do not see your question here, call or send the form above and we will answer it directly.

What does a broker do that I cannot do myself?

We negotiate with dealers and shop insurance carriers on your behalf, then hand you the numbers side by side. You skip the back and forth and the hours spent at dealerships.

Do you charge me a fee?

No hidden fees and no pressure. We work for you, not for the dealership or the insurance company.

What if my credit is not great?

We work with every credit situation, including limited or no credit history, and match you with lenders suited to your profile.

Can I get the car and the insurance together?

Yes. Handling both is what we do best, and it means your coverage is in place the day the vehicle is delivered.

Which areas do you serve?

New Jersey, New York, and Pennsylvania. Vehicles are delivered to your door.
