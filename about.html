Our Story

United Automotive Consultants is a trusted auto broker proudly serving drivers all throughout New Jersey.

Founded by a former dealership manager with over 10 years of hands-on experience, we understand exactly how frustrating and time-consuming the car-buying process can be especially when you're negotiating on your own.

That's why we built a better way: one dedicated broker who handles everything for you from start to finish.

We specialize in securing the best purchase price on the vehicle you want, while shopping top insurance carriers for the lowest possible rates. We work with every credit situation, making the process smooth and stress-free.

You'll work directly with one experienced broker who negotiates for you. Never for the dealership or insurer. No hidden fees. No pressure. Just transparent, reliable service from start to finish.
